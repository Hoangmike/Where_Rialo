MiniGame: Where is Rialo ?

Round: 0/5
Score: 0
Click "Start Round" to play
How to play:
• When you start a round, a ball will be placed under one of the three cups.
• The cups will shuffle around quickly for about 3 seconds.
• When they stop, click on the cup you think is hiding the ball.
• If correct, you earn 1 point. If wrong, no points are added.
• You have 5 rounds. Try to score as high as possible!
